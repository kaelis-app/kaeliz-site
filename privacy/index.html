01

Who we are

Kaeliz is a personal assistant app made by Kaeliz Technologies Inc., a company based in Ontario, Canada ("Kaeliz", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Kaeliz app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

02

What we collect

Your account

Your first name and email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email they share with us (Apple lets you hide your real email). Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are never visible to us.

What you add to Kaeliz

The things you type, say or save: tasks, reminders, follow-ups, lists, saved links, photos and notes, journal entries, life areas, and the people you choose to add. This is the heart of the app, and it's stored so you can see it on any device you sign in on.

What Kaeliz remembers about you

When you chat, Kaeliz may save short facts you tell it, like "my daughter starts school at 8", so it can help you better. You can see, add to, or delete every one of these in More, then About you.

Basic technical information

Our servers keep basic logs, such as the time of a request and error details, to keep Kaeliz running and secure. We don't use advertising trackers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

03

How we use it

  • To run Kaeliz: sorting what you say into tasks and lists, building your daily plan, sending reminders, and answering you in chat.
  • To remember what matters to you, so Kaeliz gets more helpful over time.
  • To keep your account secure and the app working, including fixing bugs.
  • To reply when you contact us or send feedback.

We don't sell your information, rent it, or use it for advertising.

04

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run Kaeliz for us, and only what they need:

  • Supabase stores your account and the things you add, and handles sign-in.
  • Anthropic provides the AI that sorts your brain dumps and writes chat replies. Your text is processed to produce a response and, under Anthropic's commercial terms, isn't used to train their models.
  • Apple handles speech recognition, Sign in with Apple, and app delivery. Google handles Sign in with Google if you choose it.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather and air quality for the approximate location you allow.
  • If you turn on a daily verse, the app requests it from a public scripture service. No personal information is included.

If you use a shared space, the people you invite can see what you share there. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

Our providers may store and process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law.

05

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete something, it's removed from the app. When you delete your account, your information is permanently deleted from our systems, apart from limited records we must keep by law or that remain briefly in backups before they expire.

06

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your information anytime in the app.
  • Delete what Kaeliz remembers in More, then About you.
  • Delete your account in More, then Delete account. This removes everything.
  • Turn off phone permissions in your iPhone Settings, under Kaeliz.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, at support@kaeliz.app.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under laws such as Canada's PIPEDA. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

07

Security

Your information travels over encrypted connections, and each person's data is kept private to their own account. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

08

Children

Kaeliz isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

09

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are important, we'll let you know in the app.
